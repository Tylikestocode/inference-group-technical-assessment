# Beneficiary Verification

- Procedure ID: PROC-003
- Version: 1.0
- Owner: Fictional Payments Operations
- Data label: Fictional assessment data

## When this procedure applies

Use this procedure when a transaction is held because beneficiary information
is missing, incomplete, or inconsistent with the payment instruction. Examples
include a beneficiary name, account detail, or payment reference that cannot be
verified from the available fictional records.

## Beneficiary details do not match the payment instruction

This section is the direct match for a hold recorded as “Beneficiary details do
not match the payment instruction.” Treat that reason as an unresolved
beneficiary-verification issue; it does not establish which detail is incorrect
and must not be supplemented with guessed information.

## Advisor actions

1. Confirm the transaction ID and review the recorded beneficiary mismatch.
2. Compare only the available beneficiary details with the original payment
   instruction and record which detail is missing or inconsistent.
3. If the verified records do not resolve the mismatch, keep the transaction
   held and refer the case to Fictional Payments Operations.

The advisor must not correct beneficiary details on the customer's behalf or
release, approve, reject, edit, or bypass the held transaction.

## Escalation

Escalate to Fictional Payments Operations when any beneficiary mismatch remains,
the original payment instruction is unavailable, the available information
conflicts, or verification cannot be completed. That team performs the human
review and decides whether corrected information or another controlled action
is required.
