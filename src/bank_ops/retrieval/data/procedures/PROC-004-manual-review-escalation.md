# Manual Review and Escalation

- Procedure ID: PROC-004
- Version: 1.0
- Owner: Fictional Operations Control
- Data label: Fictional assessment data

## When this procedure applies

Use this procedure as the cautious fallback when no relevant specialist
procedure can be found, verified information is incomplete or conflicting, a
required service is unavailable, or the correct next step remains uncertain.
It also applies when another procedure explicitly requires human review.

## Advisor actions

1. Confirm the transaction ID and preserve the transaction's current state.
2. Record the verified facts, unavailable information, failed service, or
   conflicting findings that prevent a reliable conclusion.
3. Send the case and the available evidence to Fictional Operations Control for
   manual review.

The advisor must not guess missing facts or release, approve, reject, edit, or
bypass the transaction while the case is unresolved.

## Escalation

Escalate to Fictional Operations Control whenever reliable guidance cannot be
produced, no relevant procedure is available, required evidence or services are
unavailable, or specialist ownership is unclear. Fictional Operations Control
assigns the case to the appropriate human review team and determines the
permitted operational outcome.
