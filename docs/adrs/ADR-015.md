# ADR-015: Keep operational decisions deterministic

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-09-06

## Context

Transaction status, escalation, and permitted actions are sensitive operational matters. They should not depend on variable model-generated text.

## Decision

Use application rules to determine escalation and allowed next steps. Use the AI model only to explain verified facts and procedure guidance in clear language.

## Consequences

- The same facts produce the same operational outcome.
- The application can prevent the AI model from releasing, approving, rejecting, or changing transactions.
- Decisions can be unit-tested without a live model.
- Business rules must be maintained explicitly as policies evolve.
